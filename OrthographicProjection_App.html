📐 Orthographic Projections (First Angle) - Problems with Stepwise Solutions

Every problem below is solved by the FIRST-ANGLE method of projection. Each solution starts from the directions of observation and the thin projection lines either side of X–Y and X₁–Y₁, sets out every feature with its dimensions before any line is drawn, and completes the views one at a time - first the whole front view, then the top view got from it by projection, then the side view got from both - giving the three views the question asks for - the LHSV in the odd-numbered problems and the RHSV in the even-numbered ones (the observer can also be changed in the panel while solving). The finished views can then be checked against a 3-D model you can rotate, zoom and pan. Each question is shown as a pictorial figure - visible edges only, with all dimensions on it and a single arrow X giving the direction of the front view. Problems 16 to 19 ask for a sectional front view: it is first drawn un-cut, with its hidden lines; the 3-D model is then shown cut on the plane, and the front view is turned into the section - hidden lines made dark, hatching added; the last two are harder objects - a bracket with a rib, a U-slot and a dovetail groove, and a bracket with an inclined arm and a boss.
