📐 Orthographic Projections (First Angle) - Problems with Stepwise Solutions

Every problem below is solved by the FIRST-ANGLE method of projection. Each solution starts from the directions of observation and the thin projection lines either side of X–Y and X₁–Y₁, sets out every feature with its dimensions before any line is drawn, and produces the front view, the top view and either the LHSV or the RHSV - whichever observer you choose in the panel while solving. The finished views can then be checked against a 3-D model you can rotate, zoom and pan. Each question is shown as a pictorial figure - visible edges only, with all dimensions on it and a single arrow X giving the direction of the front view. The last two problems ask for a sectional front view.
